personal-automation

personal-automation is a private tool that one person (GitHub user EgglestonC) uses to manage their own Google account, for example keeping their own Gmail filters and labels tidy. It is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy policy

  • The tool only accesses the Google account of its owner, who signs in to it themselves.
  • It reads message details (sender, subject, labels) and manages filters and labels in that account in order to organize it. It does not send email.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Data is processed only to carry out the owner's own automation.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions: contact EgglestonC on GitHub.
